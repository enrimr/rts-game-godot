# Civilizations

## Design Rules

- Each civ has exactly 1 unique unit (unlocked at Castle Age)
- Each civ has 1–2 unique technologies
- Bonuses must not overlap with another civ's bonus
- Missing units/techs must be compensated by bonuses elsewhere

## Civilizations

### Franks
- **Bonus**: Cavalry +20% HP; Farm upgrades free; Foragers work 15% faster
- **Unique Unit**: Throwing Axeman (infantry, high attack)
- **Unique Tech**: Bearded Axe (Throwing Axemen +1 range)
- **Missing**: Skirmisher, Camel

### Britons
- **Bonus**: Foot archers +1/+2 range (Feudal/Castle); Town Centers cost -50% wood
- **Unique Unit**: Longbowman (archer, extra range)
- **Unique Tech**: Yeomen (+1 foot archer range); Warwolf (Trebuchets 100% accuracy)
- **Missing**: Hussar, Paladin

### Mongols
- **Bonus**: Cavalry Archers fire 25% faster; Light Cavalry +30% HP
- **Unique Unit**: Mangudai (cavalry archer, anti-siege bonus damage)
- **Unique Tech**: Nomads (houses not needed for pop cap); Drill (siege workshops work 50% faster)
- **Missing**: Arbalest (fully upgraded), Castle

### Saracens
- **Bonus**: Market trade costs 5%; Archers +3 attack vs. buildings; Transport ships double capacity
- **Unique Unit**: Mameluke (camel unit, ranged)
- **Unique Tech**: Bimaristan (Mamelukes convert nearby enemies on death); Counterweights (+25% Trebuchet range)
- **Missing**: Paladin

### Byzantines
- **Bonus**: Buildings +10/20/30/40% HP per age; Counter-units cost -25%; Monks heal 2x as fast
- **Unique Unit**: Cataphract (cavalry, trample damage)
- **Unique Tech**: Logistica (Cataphracts +6 trample damage); Greek Fire (Fire Ships +1 range)
- **Missing**: Paladin, Hussars
