A classic RTS set in the Canary archipelago: native peoples, European powers and two legendary Atlantic civilizations compete over islands of volcanoes, laurisilva and trade winds. Gather, advance through the ages, train an army and eliminate your rivals — or win by Wonder or Regicide.

Economy and ages

Population starts at 15 and every House adds +5. You advance through four ages — Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial — by paying resources and waiting out a timer; each age unlocks units, buildings and technologies (32 in total, spread across eight buildings: Blacksmith, University, Temple, Barracks, Stable, Mill and the two gathering camps).

The 8 civilizations

Each civilization is recognisable at a glance by its architecture (material and roof silhouette) and the dress of its units; colour only marks the player. Each one has passive bonuses, a unique unit and a hero with an ability of their own.

Guanches — the lords of the volcano

Defensive · volcanic terrain · infantry

  • Cross the malpaís without penalty (everyone else crawls)
  • Stone buildings +20% HP · pikemen from the Dark Age
  • Immune to the Calima; half Volcanic Ash penalty

Unique: Menceyes Guard (heavy anti-cavalry infantry) · Hero: Bencomo (+30% attack speed in an area)

Canarii — the ravine economy

Economic boom · cheap archers · high ground

  • Villagers gather food +15% · houses serve as drop-off points
  • Archers -20% food cost
  • Half Sea Fog and Trade Winds penalty

Unique: Ravine Archer (+2 range next to cliffs) · Hero: Doramas (forces an enemy to attack only him)

Mahos — the dune riders

Raiding · fast cavalry · cheap buildings

  • Buildings -30% wood (dry stone)
  • Scouts and light cavalry +25% speed
  • +40% vision on the dunes · they cross them without slowing · half Calima penalty

Unique: Sand Raider (hit-and-run light cavalry) · Hero: Guadarfía

Francos — the feudal charge

Cavalry · farms · cheap ages

  • Advancing an age costs -15%
  • Cavalry +15% HP from the Feudal Age

Unique: Chevalier Normand (medium cavalry, a charge with extra damage) · Hero: Jean de Béthencourt

Britanos — the English reach

Elite archers · naval pressure

  • Foot archers +1 range per age (from Feudal)
  • Warships attack +20% faster
  • Half Atlantic Storm penalty

Unique: Longbowman (exceptional range, +4 vs cavalry) · Hero: Francis Drake

Castellanos — the late-game power

Long game · free technology · full roster

  • Each age advance unlocks a free Blacksmith technology
  • Swordsmen +15% HP
  • Half Calima penalty

Unique: Conquistador (armoured infantry with Salvo Fire: 3 rapid shots) · Hero: Don Quijote de la Mancha

Atlantes — the legendary amphibians

Coast · fast ships · amphibious

  • +50% vision within 400 px of a shore · ships attack 20% faster
  • Their Tidecaller wades shallow water at full speed and swims the open sea (at 60%)
  • Immune to the Atlantic Storm; in Sea Fog they are twice as hard to spot

Unique: Tidecaller (amphibious infantry with an area Tidal Pulse) · Hero: Artaxerax

Fenicios — the merchants of the sea

Trade · cheap fleet · naval mobility

  • Market from the Dark Age · Triremes generate passive gold (5 gold every 30 s)
  • Ships −15% cost · Market mercenaries cost them 25% less
  • Half Atlantic Storm penalty

Unique: Trireme (ramming galley: ×2 vs ships + knockback) · Hero: Hanno the Navigator

Weather is procedural and announced in advance: Calima (vision and gathering), Atlantic Storm (naval and fishing), Sea Fog (coastal concealment), Trade Winds (naval speed by heading, projectile drift) and Volcanic Ash (damage to buildings near calderas, Volcanic Coast only). Each civilization better resists the weather of its homeland.

Three unit styles

From Settings → Display you choose the visual style of every unit; the change applies instantly, mid-match. Classic is the original flat vector figures, Enhanced adds outlines, shading and extra animation, and Redesigned remodels every figure faithful to Canarian lore.

More screenshots in the galleries: unit-redesign-gallery · unit-enhanced-gallery.

Maps and setup

5 map types (Plains, Standard, Volcanic Coast, Desert Coast, Islands — the last one demands a navy and transports), 4 resource modes (Scarce→Full Combat), 3 victory modes, configurable weather frequency, teams 1–4 and a choice of civilization and hero gender in the lobby. Games can be saved in 99 slots.

Beyond the skirmish: a campaign about the Atlantean invasion (prologue + 4 missions), LAN/Internet multiplayer for up to 4 players with online save and resume, and replays of every match, exportable to video with a cinematic mode.
