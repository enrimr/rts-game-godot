# The Harimaguada — Healer of the Archipelago

## Historical Context

The harimaguadas (also *maguadas* or *magadas*) were consecrated women of
pre-conquest Gran Canaria. Chronicles describe them living in community near
sacred precincts, dressed in long white leather tunics, keepers of ritual,
healing and rain-prayer. They enjoyed a protected, near-inviolable status in
Canarii society: their persons and their sanctuaries stood outside the feuds
of chiefdoms.

## In the Game

**Why every civilization trains her:** the war for the archipelago is fought
ON the islands — and the harimaguadas belong to the land, not to any banner.
Foreign powers do not bring healers across the ocean: they raise a temple on
Canarian soil, and it is the local priestesses who tend any army that
respects their shrines. Fight under whichever flag you like; the islands and
their people remain Canarian.

**Role:** trained at the Temple (Castle Age). She never fights — attack
zero, passive by vow. Given a patient she follows the wounded and mends them
on the field; left to herself she runs her own triage, tending the most
wounded ally nearby, worst first. The Temple itself is her greater work: a
ward where sheltered units heal (heroes at half pace — even a king waits his
turn under her roof).

**Voice:** every civilization pronounces her calling in its own
synthesized tongue — same woman, eight accents.
