## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** The Age of Empires II you never played: the conquest of the
Canary Islands, where weather is a weapon, dogs herd stolen sheep, and an
invisible fleet hides in the sea fog.

**Genre shelf:** Classic 2D RTS (base-building, four resources, four ages),
positioned deliberately as *AoE2-like* — that term converts searches.

**What no competitor can claim:**

| Hook | Why it sells |
|---|---|
| Canary Islands, 15th century | A setting NO strategy game has touched: Guanches vs Castilian conquest, real lore (almogarén shrines, Harimaguada priestesses, Presa Canario dogs, tabona cursors) |
| Weather as a strategic layer | Calima dust, Atlantic storms, sea fog cloaking, volcanic ash that burns bases near calderas — with per-civ resistances |
| The pastoral duel | Herding dogs race the enemy's dogs for wild sheep; enemy flocks convert on contact — an economy skirmish before the first sword is drawn |
| Amphibious Atlantes | A civ whose unique unit swims, whose fleet is half-invisible in sea fog |
| 100 % procedural everything | Zero art/audio assets: sprites, animations, voices (each civ speaks its own synthesized language), music — a technical story devlogs and press love |
| Creator kit built in | Replays export to video with cinematic camera, A/B clip marks, minimap toggle — content creators are first-class users |

**Primary audiences, in order:**
1. Spanish-speaking AoE2 community (Spain + LatAm) — the setting is *theirs*,
   and AoE2's biggest streamers are Spanish-speaking.
2. AoE2/RTS nostalgia players worldwide (the "AoE2-like" search).
3. Canary Islands local press/identity — free earned media (national coverage
   of "the game about the conquest of the Canaries").
4. Godot/indie-dev community — the procedural pipeline story.

## 5. Rollout plan

**Phase 0 — now (pre-beta):**
- Steam page live with capsule, 6+ screenshots, teaser (30 s cut of the
  trailer). The wishlist counter starts here; nothing else matters until the
  page exists.
- Discord server + a pinned "beta sign-up" channel.

**Phase 1 — public beta (first 4–6 weeks):**
- Beta keys to 10–15 Spanish-speaking AoE2 creators (Spain + LatAm). Pitch:
  "an AoE2-like about the conquest of the Canaries, you can export your best
  moments to video from inside the game." The creator kit IS the pitch.
- Canary Islands press kit (ES): local angle first (Canarias7, La Provincia,
  RTVC), then national gaming press (Vandal, 3DJuegos, MeriStation).
- Godot devlog #1: "An RTS with zero asset files" (procedural sprites/voices) —
  r/godot, Godot showcase, Hacker News.
- Weekly beta patch notes posted as Steam news — visible momentum.

**Phase 2 — Steam Next Fest (the wishlist engine):**
- Enter the first Next Fest AFTER the beta has hardened the demo. Demo scope:
  campaign mission 1 + skirmish on 2 map types, 4 civs.
- Full trailer premieres here. Creators re-activated with demo tournament
  (small prize, bracket streamed).

**Phase 3 — launch:**
- Launch when wishlists ≥ 7–10 k (below that, Early Access instead).
- Launch discount 10 %, creator day-one embargo lift, "the making of the
  voices" devlog as launch-week content.

**Cadence content (all phases):** one clip per week from the community's own
replays (the A/B export exists precisely for this), #ScreenshotSaturday with
harness output, civ-spotlight threads (8 civs = 8 weeks of content).

## 6. Asset pipeline

All commands run from `project/`, `$GODOT` = engine binary. Output lands in
`builds/marketing/` (gitignored — assets are artifacts, the pipeline is the
source).

```sh
# Beauty screenshots from any replay: no HUD, fog revealed, camera auto-framed
# on battles (closest hostile contact) or, failing that, touring each player's
# town. Times via CALIMA_MKT_TIMES="330,660,4303" or default spread.
CALIMA_CINE=1 CALIMA_MKT_ZOOM=1.6 \
CALIMA_MKT_REPLAY="user://replays/<file>.calrep" \
CALIMA_SHOT_DIR=/tmp/calima-marketing \
$GODOT --path . --resolution 1920x1080 res://tools/check_marketing_shots.tscn

# Find the battles first (prints closest-contact distance + army sizes over time):
CALIMA_CINE=1 CALIMA_MKT_SCAN=1 CALIMA_MKT_STEP=300 \
CALIMA_MKT_REPLAY="user://replays/<file>.calrep" \
$GODOT --path . --resolution 640x360 res://tools/check_marketing_shots.tscn

# Video clip of a chosen window, camera framed on the action:
CALIMA_REPLAY="user://replays/<file>.calrep" CALIMA_CINE=1 \
CALIMA_REPLAY_FROM=330 CALIMA_REPLAY_TO=415 CALIMA_CINE_CAM="-1169,733,1.5" \
$GODOT --path . --resolution 1920x1080 \
  --write-movie ../builds/marketing/clip.avi --fixed-fps 30
```

`CALIMA_CINE_CAM="x,y[,zoom]"` frames export runs (world coordinates; get them
from the scan line `mid=(x, y)`). In-game, the replay bar's A/B marks + clip
button do the same export interactively.

AVI output is MJPEG — transcode for the web with
`ffmpeg -i clip.avi -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p clip.mp4`.
