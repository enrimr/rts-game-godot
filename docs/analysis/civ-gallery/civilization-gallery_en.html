Per-civilization identity: silhouette and material (roof shape, walls, headgear) — player colour only marks ownership. Buildings: top row completed, bottom row under construction (scaffolding). Units: shared template (villager → cavalry) in the civ's dress, plus its unique unit at the end. Regenerable with tools/check_building_gallery.tscn and tools/check_unit_gallery.tscn (CALIMA_CIV=…).

Guanches

Dark volcanic stone · flat roof with parapet · cream trim — builders of the malpaís. Units wear a headband. Unique: Menceyes Guard.

Bonuses: stone buildings +20% HP (walls and mining camps) · pikemen available from the Dark Age · they cross the malpaís, impassable to everyone else · immune to the Calima and half Volcanic Ash penalty.

Canarii

Whitewash · green hipped roof · wood — villages of the laurisilva. No headgear; identity in the sash. Unique: Ravine Archer.

Bonuses: food gathering +15% · the whole archer line costs 20% less food · Houses serve as resource drop-off points · half Sea Fog and Trade Winds penalty.

Mahos

Sand adobe · dome with finial · ochre — desert of the eastern islands. Turban with a reddish tail. Unique: Sand Raider.

Bonuses: buildings −30% wood cost · Scout and Sand Raider +25% speed · +40% vision on the dunes, which they cross without penalty · half Calima penalty.

Francos

Cream timber framing · steep slate gable · ridge beam — continental feudal. Visored cap. Unique: Chevalier Normand.

Bonuses: advancing an age costs 15% less · cavalry +15% HP · farms are built 20% faster.

Britanos

Grey ashlar · near-black charcoal gable — northern keeps. A hood framing the face. Unique: Longbowman.

Bonuses: foot archers +1 range per age (from Feudal) · warships +20% attack speed · half Atlantic Storm penalty.

Castellanos

Cream stucco · terracotta hipped roof · terracotta capitals — Iberian plateau. A thin helmet crest. Unique: Conquistador.

Bonuses: a free Blacksmith technology with each age advance · swordsman line +15% HP · towers and a garrisoned TC shoot 10% further · half Calima penalty.

Atlantes

Pale greenish stone · stepped bronze roof · turquoise band — the mythical ones. Bronze diadem with a gem. Unique: Tidecaller.

Bonuses: +50% vision within 400 px of a shore · ships +20% attack speed · in Sea Fog they are spotted at half distance (90 px) · their Tidecaller is amphibious (shallow water at full speed, ocean at 60%) · immune to the Atlantic Storm and half Sea Fog penalty.

Fenicios

Sandstone · flat roof with a purple wash · purple awnings — traders of the Levant. Headscarf with a purple tail. Unique: Trireme.

Bonuses: Market available from the Dark Age · ships −15% cost · Triremes generate passive gold (5 gold every 30 s) · Market mercenaries 25% cheaper · half Atlantic Storm penalty.

Validation: a blind critic identified 4/4 civilizations from unlabelled screenshots using only the motif legend (the two confusable pairs it flagged — Britons/Franks and Fenicios/Guanches — are already fixed with charcoal and the purple wash). Styles defined in scripts/utils/civ_style.gd; tweaking a civ is one line. The listed bonuses were verified against resources/civilizations/*.tres and their readers (CivBonusManager, FogOfWar, TerrainManager, Trireme, House, Barracks, Market).

All art is procedural Polygon2D, original to the project.
